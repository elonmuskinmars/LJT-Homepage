I am a first-year PhD candidate at [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.ust.hk/), advised by Professor Junxian He. I previously attended Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) as an undergraduate student.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My specific research interests include LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM), and LLM truthfulness and Interpretability.

I am currently working as a Research Intern at MINIMAX. Previously, I was a Research Intern at Tencent WXG (advised by Zifei Shan) and Shanghai AI Lab (advised by Prof. Yu Cheng).
